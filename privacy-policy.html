Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At our software and digital agency, we deeply value the trust you place in us when collaborating on your web applications, software systems, and business growth strategies. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you work with us or visit our digital platforms.

1. Information We Collect

  • Project & Client Data: Information shared during consultations, technical scoping, and development (such as brand assets, business requirements, and integration credentials).
  • Communication Records: Emails, chat logs, and meeting notes exchanged during the lifecycle of your project.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Standard analytics, cookies, and diagnostic information collected when interacting with our website or client portals.

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the data we gather strictly for professional and operational purposes:

  • To design, develop, deploy, and maintain your software and web applications.
  • To communicate project milestones, technical support, and invoicing updates.
  • To improve our agency services and internal development workflows.

We never sell, rent, or trade your proprietary business data, code, or customer lists to any third parties.

3. Intellectual Property & Confidentiality

All source code, custom UI/UX designs, database configurations, and proprietary workflows built specifically for your project remain strictly confidential. Upon full project completion and settlement, full ownership of the deliverables is transferred to you. Our team treats all unreleased business secrets and strategy documents under strict non-disclosure standards.

4. Data Security Measures

We implement robust technical protocols to secure your project environment, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/SSL), secure environment variables for API keys and payment gateways, and role-based access control across our version control and hosting infrastructures (such as GitHub, Vercel, and Supabase).

5. Third-Party Services

To deliver high-performance solutions, we utilize trusted enterprise-grade infrastructure and third-party tools (such as secure cloud hosting providers, database management tools, and verified payment processors like Stripe). These services maintain their own independent privacy frameworks and security compliances.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please feel free to reach out to us directly through our official communication channels or via email.
